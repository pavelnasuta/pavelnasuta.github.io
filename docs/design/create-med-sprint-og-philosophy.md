# Clinical Route

Clinical Route treats a learning sprint as a measured path rather than a startup spectacle. The composition is divided between a deep blue-green field of orientation and a quiet paper-coloured working surface. A single coral signal marks the current position: not an alarm, but the point where observation becomes a decision.

Space carries the method. The left edge works as a vertical route map: five disciplined stations, a precise line, one emphasized node. The large field is deliberately spare, so the title behaves like the principal artefact on a laboratory workbench rather than a marketing headline.

Colour is restricted to ink blue-green, off-white paper, teal for verified information, and one coral decision marker. The palette should feel rigorous and human—closer to a carefully prepared teaching handout than a generic health-tech interface. Every line and gap must look meticulously calibrated.

Typography pairs a high-contrast editorial serif for the sprint name with compact utilitarian uppercase labels for the process. Words remain sparse: the image must communicate through the physical rhythm of five stations, not explanatory copy. It should read immediately at small social-card size and reward closer inspection with master-level alignment.

The result is a painstakingly made visual field for evidence-led work: quiet, exact, and memorable without decorative noise.
